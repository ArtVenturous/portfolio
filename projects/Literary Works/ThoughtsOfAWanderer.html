Thoughts of a Wanderer

The way of the world is not right. Not that is wrong but... People do the things they do because they are told so and... Later they come to love it and believe it is what they want and should do.

Everything makes sense, but nothing makes sense in reality. Mankind wants and tries to make sense of it. The very little sense they could make, they make people believe what they want them to believe.

...so what is the truth? No one knows for sure. The truth Mankind has come to know may not be the truth at all.

We are but puppets. The puppeteer or the puppeteers control what we do, say, think, and feel. They decide what will happen to us... and there's nothing we could do about it.

There is no real freedom, but we do experience or exhibit at least a little freedom.
